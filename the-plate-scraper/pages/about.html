Why “The Plate Scraper”?

Because the best part of a great meal is what you scrape off the plate.

The Plate Scraper is a recipe site built for real weeknights. Every recipe in the library is tested, timed, and written in plain English — then the site does the busywork around it: scale the servings to your table in one tap, swap any ingredient with an exact ratio from our Substitution Guide, and send the whole list to a shopping list that organizes itself by aisle.

Where the recipes come from

Some recipes are original to our kitchen. Others we find on the recipe blogs we follow, cook, tweak, and publish on the blog — always credited to the source. It's the same way home cooks have worked for generations: find a good dish, make it yours, keep what works.

What's in the kitchen

  • 📖 Recipe library — tested weeknight cooking, searchable by category or ingredient
  • 🔁 Substitution Guide — 26 staples with exact ratios and honest notes
  • 🛒 Shopping List — aisle-grouped, quantities merged, syncs to your account
  • 👩‍ Members area — saved recipes, your lists, and the good stuff first
  • 🍳 Kitchen Gear — the exact tools behind the recipes
