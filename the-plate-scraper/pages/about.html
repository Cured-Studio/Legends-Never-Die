Why “The Plate Scraper”?

Because the best part of a great meal is what you scrape off the plate. The name stuck, and the tool followed: a home-cooking platform built around one belief — the best recipe on the internet should take 10 seconds to get into your kitchen.

So we built the scraper first. Paste a URL, get a clean card you own. Then everything a real kitchen needs around it: a substitution guide with honest ratios, a shopping list that groups by aisle and merges quantities, a members area that remembers your scrapes, and a Feed Room that follows your favorite recipe blogs and rewrites their posts into your own voice — always with credit where it's due.

The tools, in one breath

  • 🧲 Recipe Scraper — URL or pasted HTML/text → your recipe card
  • 📡 Feed Room — RSS reader + house-style rewriter for recipe blogs
  • 🔁 Substitution Guide — 26 staples, exact ratios
  • 🛒 Shopping List — aisle-grouped, syncs to your account
  • 👩‍🍳 Members Area — saved recipes, scrape history, tiers
  • 🎛 Affiliate Control Panel — stores, tracked links, revenue dashboard
  • 🗄 MySQL Wizard — point the whole thing at a real database, in five steps

Running it yourself

The server is plain Python (standard library only — it runs anywhere). Want MySQL? On Windows, one script does it all:

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\setup-theplatescraper.ps1

…or finish it by hand in the browser at /setup.html (the MySQL Wizard) and keep the JSON file as a hot backup. The whole site lives at theplatescraper.com when you're ready to point the domain at it.

The Plate Scraper is an independent project. Recipes in the library are original to our kitchen; content rewritten in the Feed Room always credits its source. Some links are affiliate links, and that's a proud, advertised part of the plan.
